Dual-Vector Cybersecurity Analysis

ScamLens inspects both textual semantic manipulation and deceptive digital link structures.

Context-Aware Message Scanning

NLP & Semantic Meaning Engine

Our Java detection engine evaluates intent, grammatical negation, and contextual urgency instead of naive keyword matching. It understands that "Never share your OTP" is a safety advisory, while "Share your OTP immediately" is a critical threat.

  • Covers SMS, WhatsApp, Email, and Social Media messages.
  • Detects 7 threat categories including Job Scams and Financial Fraud.
  • Extracts exact suspicious phrases with contextual explanations.

Safe Passive URL Inspection

Zero-Execution Deep Link Analysis

ScamLens uses Java's native java.net.URI and java.net.IDN parsing to analyze link architecture safely without ever executing code, downloading files, or visiting malicious servers.

  • Unmasks URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl, t.co, is.gd).
  • Identifies direct IP hosts, suspicious userinfo (@ syntax), and Punycode.
  • Flags excessive subdomains and brand impersonation in domain paths.
